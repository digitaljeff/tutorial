## The seven gaps Backlot exploits

1. **Nobody owns the full loop.** Tools without audiences (LTX, Hedra, InVideo, Saga) or audience plays without creation tools (Holywater, ReelShort). Showrunner is closest but Discord-gated with no real platform. **Script → episode → channel → subscribers in one product is open.**
2. **Sora's death is the wedge narrative.** Infinite unstructured AI video failed economically; what retains is *serialized characters and stories* — exactly the show-bible model. The $11B microdrama market proves people pay for cheap serialized video.
3. **Script-first is underserved.** Everyone is prompt-first or storyboard-first; nobody ingests a screenplay and returns a coherent multi-scene episode with a persistent cast.
4. **Cartoon/stylized = cost moat.** Stylized looks tolerate cheap/open models (LTX-2 ~$0.002/megapixel; ~$0.01–0.02/s true cost) → 70%+ margins where realistic-video rivals bleed on Veo-class rates. Informs default style presets.
5. **Remix rev-share:** announced by Showrunner, shipped by nobody. Our Phase 3 hook.
6. **Compliance as a feature:** C2PA + AI labels + neutral age gate baked in from day one (EU AI Act Art. 50 applies **from Aug 2, 2026**; COPPA amendments fully enforceable since Apr 2026; Apple UGC rules require report/block/24h takedown). Cheap at design time, expensive to retrofit — most rivals haven't.
7. **Pricing trust:** transparent per-episode quotes + rollover credits vs. the industry's hated expiring credit pools.

## Positioning statement

**For creators who want to run a show, not prompt a clip.** Backlot is the studio: persistent cast, sets, voices, and season arcs that turn ideas into publishable episodes — and the network where those shows find their audience.

## What we deliberately don't compete on

- Photorealistic cinematic film quality (LTX/Veo hero-shot territory) — we optimize for *watchable serialized comedy* at sustainable cost.
- General-purpose video editing — no timeline editor beyond retakes/swaps in v1.
- Open model zoo access — curated stack, quality-tuned presets, no 200-model dropdown.
